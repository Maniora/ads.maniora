// COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Q // How much does a website cost?

Every project is priced based on what actually needs to be built. Most websites fall between ₹30,000 and ₹2,00,000, depending on the number of pages, custom functionality, and integrations. We agree on the price before development starts, so there are no hourly charges or surprise invoices.

Q // How long does it take?

Most landing pages are completed within 1–2 weeks. Business websites usually take 2–4 weeks, depending on the amount of content, revisions, and integrations. We'll give you a realistic timeline before we begin.

Q // Do you provide hosting?

Yes. We can deploy your website on platforms like Vercel, Netlify, or your own VPS. You own the hosting account, domains, and source code—we don't lock clients into our infrastructure.

Q // Do you build websites with SEO in mind?

We build every website with technical SEO best practices: semantic HTML, fast loading, proper metadata, responsive layouts, and clean page structure. While no agency can guarantee Google rankings, we make sure your website gives search engines the best possible foundation.

Q // Can I edit content myself?

Yes. If your website includes editable content, we'll build it so you can update text, images, blog posts, or products yourself without writing code.

Q // Do I own the website after it's completed?

Yes. Once the project is finished and paid for, you own the website, source code, domain, hosting account, and content.

Q // Can you integrate payment gateways or WhatsApp?

Yes. We regularly integrate Razorpay, Stripe, WhatsApp, booking forms, contact forms, Google Maps, analytics, and other third-party services when required.

Q // What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's completely fine. Most clients come to us with a business problem, not a technical solution. We'll help identify whether you need a landing page, a full website, automation, or sometimes nothing at all.
